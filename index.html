Our Story

Live the
Caribbean Soul.

The heart of Saint Martin beats right here. Located just past the Sandy Ground bridge, Triple R is where the island comes alive. Picture the perfect mix: hypnotic DJ sets blending with the irresistible aroma of masterful Caribbean cuisine.

It's the ultimate spot to chill, eat well, and soak in the true island vibe. From oxtail to fresh crepes, from aged rum to craft cocktails — every moment here is a memory in the making.
