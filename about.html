The Origin

Where the Island
Comes Alive

Triple R Bar Restaurant was born in 2017 in the heart of Sandy Ground, just after the bridge — the soul of Saint Martin's most vibrant village. It began with a simple but powerful vision: create a space where the true spirit of the Caribbean could breathe, gather, and celebrate.

The founders — deeply rooted in island culture — wanted more than a restaurant. They envisioned a living room for the community. A place where the smell of authentic Caribbean cooking mingles with the bass of a DJ set as the sun melts into the lagoon. Where locals and visitors sit side by side, strangers become friends, and every evening feels like a celebration.

From the very first night, Triple R became something the island had been waiting for. A space that did not try to imitate anywhere else — rooted entirely in Saint Martin's own flavors, rhythms, and warmth.

Our Journey

The Timeline

2017

The Beginning

Triple R opens its doors in Sandy Ground, just after the bridge. A humble space with a big dream — bring the true soul of the Caribbean to a single table.

2021

First DJ Nights

The terrace fills up every Friday. Weekly DJ sets launch, turning Triple R from a restaurant into an event. The island starts talking.

2022

Menu Expansion

The kitchen deepens its roots. New creole dishes, a cocktail menu developed in-house, and weekend brunch become instant island favorites.

2023

Sandy Ground's Spot

Triple R earns its title as the go-to spot in Sandy Ground. Word spreads beyond the island — travelers plan visits to Saint Martin around a night here.

2024

Renovation & Reinvention

A full interior renovation reimagines the space. New lighting, refined decor, and an expanded bar elevate the experience without losing the soul that made it.

2025 — Now

A New Generation

The story continues. Triple R grows into its next chapter — new events, new collaborations, and the same unwavering commitment to island authenticity. The best is still ahead.
